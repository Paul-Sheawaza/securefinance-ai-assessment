# Scope and assumptions

These are analytical assumptions, not established facts. If they change, the legal conclusions (especially on the EU AI Act) change with them.

- **Jurisdiction:** SecureFinance Africa initially operates in Nigeria only. No EU establishment, EU market placement or use of its AI outputs in the EU is assumed at launch; this is reassessed before expansion.
- **Data subjects:** Adult customers in Nigeria. EU residents are assumed only after expansion.
- **System use:** Identity verification is a one-to-one selfie match against a claimed identity (biometric data involved). Fraud detection flags suspicious activity. Financial risk assessment informs credit decisions or limits for individuals.
- **Human review:** Recommendations receive meaningful human review before any consequential adverse decision.
- **Regulatory status:** SecureFinance is assumed to be a data controller "of major importance" under the NDPA 2023, pending the Commission's thresholds.
- **Fairness data:** All fairness analysis uses a fictional dataset, fictional groups and a constructed ground truth. No real customer records are used anywhere in this repository.
